About FitWiki

A student project that aims to make fitness knowledge free and simple for everyone.

What is FitWiki?

FitWiki is a free fitness encyclopedia website. We cover workouts, nutrition, supplements, anatomy, and more. No memberships, no ads, just information. This website was made as a web development mini project by second semester B.Tech Computer Engineering students at NMIMS.
