Beginner's Guide

Never been to a gym before? This covers everything you need to know to start.

1. Your First Day

Walking into a gym for the first time can feel intimidating. Everyone seems to know what they are doing. The truth is everyone was a beginner once. Nobody is watching you. Focus on yourself.

On your first day, don't try to do everything. Pick 4 or 5 basic exercises, use light weight, and focus only on learning proper form. Form before weight, always.

2. How to Warm Up

Never skip the warm up. Cold muscles are more prone to injury. A good warm up takes 5 to 10 minutes.

  • 5 minutes of light cardio — walk, cycle, or skip rope
  • Dynamic stretches — arm circles, leg swings, hip rotations
  • 1 or 2 light sets of your first exercise with very low weight

3. Understanding Reps and Sets

A rep is one complete movement of an exercise. A set is a group of reps done together. Three sets of ten reps means doing ten reps, resting, and repeating that three times total.

  • For strength — 3 to 5 reps with heavy weight
  • For muscle building — 8 to 12 reps with moderate weight
  • For endurance — 15 to 20 reps with lighter weight

As a beginner, just do 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps for all exercises.

4. Choosing a Workout Split

A split is how you divide training across the week. As a beginner the best option is Full Body 3 times per week — Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Train every muscle group each session and rest on the other days.

Avoid complicated splits until you have been training consistently for at least 3 months.

5. Basic Nutrition

You don't need a perfect diet to start. Focus on these basics:

  • Eat enough protein — at least 1.6g per kg of your bodyweight daily
  • Don't skip meals — your body needs fuel to train well
  • Drink 3 to 4 litres of water daily
  • Eat mostly whole foods — rice, dal, eggs, chicken, vegetables, fruits
  • You don't need supplements to start. Food always comes first.

6. Rest and Recovery

This is the most ignored part of fitness. Muscles don't grow during workouts — they grow while you sleep. Without enough rest, you won't make progress no matter how hard you train.

  • Sleep 7 to 9 hours every night
  • Don't train the same muscle two days in a row
  • Take at least 1 to 2 rest days per week
  • Muscle soreness after training is normal — light movement helps

7. Gym Etiquette

  • Put weights back where you found them after use
  • Wipe equipment down after sweating on it
  • Don't sit on a machine between sets during busy hours
  • Ask before working in on a machine someone is using
  • Keep phone calls outside the gym

8. Most Common Beginner Mistakes

  • Going too heavy too soon — this is how injuries happen
  • Always skipping leg day
  • Being inconsistent — 3 months of steady training beats 3 weeks of intense effort
  • Comparing yourself to others in the gym
  • Buying supplements before fixing your diet
