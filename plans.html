Workout Plans

Weekly training schedules for different experience levels.

Full Body — 3 Days per Week

Good for beginners. Train every other day so the body can rest properly. Focus on learning form before adding weight.

Monday
Full Body A — Squat, Bench Press, Bent-Over Row, Plank (3 sets of 10)
Tuesday
Rest or light walk
Wednesday
Full Body B — Deadlift, Overhead Press, Lat Pulldown, Crunches (3 sets of 10)
Thursday
Rest or light walk
Friday
Full Body A — Repeat Monday, try to add small weight if possible
Sat-Sun
Rest and recovery

Upper Lower Split — 4 Days per Week

Trains each muscle group twice a week. Good after 2 to 3 months of beginner training.

Monday
Upper A — Bench Press, Row, Overhead Press, Bicep Curl, Tricep Dip (4x8)
Tuesday
Lower A — Squat, Romanian Deadlift, Leg Press, Calf Raise, Plank (4x8)
Wednesday
Rest or light cardio
Thursday
Upper B — Incline Press, Pull-Ups, Lateral Raise, Hammer Curl (4x10)
Friday
Lower B — Deadlift, Leg Curl, Lunges, Ab Circuit (4x10)
Sat-Sun
Rest and recovery

Push Pull Legs — 6 Days per Week

Popular program for experienced lifters. Each muscle group is trained twice per week.

Monday
Push — Bench Press, Incline Press, Overhead Press, Lateral Raise, Tricep Pushdown
Tuesday
Pull — Deadlift, Pull-Ups, Seated Row, Face Pull, Bicep Curl
Wednesday
Legs — Squat, Leg Press, Romanian Deadlift, Leg Curl, Calf Raise, Abs
Thursday
Push — Repeat with heavier weight or different variations
Friday
Pull — Repeat with heavier weight or different variations
Saturday
Legs — Repeat with heavier weight or different variations
Sunday
Rest and recovery
