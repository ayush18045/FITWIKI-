Supplements Info

What common supplements are, what they do, and who should take them.

Disclaimer: This page is for information only. Supplements are not magic pills. Fix your diet first. Consult a doctor before starting any supplement.

🥛 Whey Protein

What it is: Fast-digesting protein from milk.

What it does: Helps hit daily protein target. Good post-workout.

Who needs it: Anyone struggling to get enough protein from food.

⚗️ Creatine

What it is: Natural compound found in meat and fish.

What it does: Increases strength and power. Helps do 1-2 more reps per set.

Who needs it: Anyone doing strength training. Most proven supplement.

💪 BCAA

What it is: Three amino acids — Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine.

What it does: Reduces muscle breakdown during training.

Who needs it: Not needed if your diet already has enough protein.

⚡ Pre-Workout

What it is: Mix of caffeine and other stimulants.

What it does: Boosts energy and focus during training.

Who needs it: Use carefully. Can cause sleep issues if overused.

🐟 Omega-3

What it is: Essential fatty acids from fish oil.

What it does: Reduces inflammation, supports heart and joints.

Who needs it: Everyone, especially those who don't eat fish regularly.

💊 Multivitamins

What it is: Mix of vitamins and minerals in one tablet.

What it does: Fills nutritional gaps in your diet.

Who needs it: People who don't eat a wide variety of foods.
